## After clusters kept their resolved parts

A cluster used to store its parts' spellings and nothing else, so scoring one
re-resolved each part: up to six full runs of the resolution seam for a single
comparison of two clusters. The parts now carry the features the synthesizer
already worked out for them.

The trade is an allocation on the resolution side against those re-resolutions
on the scoring side. Storing a part resolved from an inventory costs one small
array copy, because the array the lookup filled is stack scratch; a synthesized
part's array moves across and costs nothing.

Measured on the same corpus and machine, three runs each, before and after:

tokenize    50.5 -> 50.1, 50.7, 51.6 us/token   (the allocation; no movement)
distance    20.2 -> 17.2, 17.7, 18.5 us/pair    (the re-resolutions; ~13% off)

Tokenization is where the added allocation lands and it did not move, because
only a cluster pays it and most tokens are not clusters. The pair figure is the
one the change was for. Both differ from the numbers above, which were recorded
on other hardware; what is comparable is the before-and-after pair, taken
together.
